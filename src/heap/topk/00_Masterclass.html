HeapTop-K

Heap · Top-K — Masterclass

PriorityQueue tricks: maintaining the top-k items, merging k streams, two-heap medians, lazy removal — all rest on the heap's O(log n) push/pop.

Jargon

Min-heap of size k
For "k LARGEST": keep the k largest seen by evicting min when full. Peek = kth largest.
Max-heap of size k
For "k SMALLEST": symmetric — peek = kth smallest.
Two-Heap Median
Max-heap (low half) + min-heap (high half), balanced by size.
Lazy Removal
Skip stale top entries (already removed by index) lazily on each pop.
Custom Comparator
(count desc, lex asc) etc. — Heap by composite key.
