P17 — Egg Drop

Hard • LC #887

Pattern (Inverted DP)

// dp[k][m] = max floors solvable with k eggs and m moves.
// dp[k][m] = dp[k-1][m-1] + dp[k][m-1] + 1.
// Find smallest m with dp[k][m] >= n.
Insight: Flip "floors → moves" to "moves → floors." The recurrence becomes additive and the answer reduces to a search over m.
