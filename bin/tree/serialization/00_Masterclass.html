TreeSerialization

Tree · Serialization & Construction — Masterclass

Encoding and decoding trees, plus building trees from different representations (preorder + inorder, sorted arrays, parsed strings).

Jargon

Pre-order Serialize
"root left right" walk with null markers; uniquely identifies any tree.
Subtree Signature
Canonical string per subtree; lets you bucket duplicates in a HashMap.
BST Bounds
(min, max) interval narrowing as you descend — only constraint needed to validate or build a BST.
Threading
Reusing pointers (e.g., right) to chain nodes in a non-standard order.
