P02 — Burst Balloons

Hard • LC #312

Pattern (Last balloon)

// Pad with 1 on both sides. dp[i][j] = max coins bursting all balloons in (i,j) exclusive.
// k = LAST balloon burst in (i,j) ⇒ its neighbours at that moment are i and j.
// dp[i][j] = max over k of dp[i][k] + a[i]*a[k]*a[j] + dp[k][j].
Insight: Asking "which balloon do I burst LAST" makes its neighbours fixed (the interval endpoints), removing the otherwise-tangled dependency on prior bursts.
