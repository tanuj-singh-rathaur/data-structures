P11 — Minimum Cost to Cut a Stick

Hard • LC #1547

Pattern

// Sort cuts; pad with 0 and n.
// dp[i][j] = min cost to perform every cut in (i,j) ⇒
//   dp[i][j] = min over k in (i,j) of dp[i][k] + dp[k][j] + (cuts[j] - cuts[i]).
Insight: Each cut splits the current stick; the cost is the CURRENT stick length, not the original — that's the (cuts[j]-cuts[i]) term.
